Privacy Policy — Habiterum

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Habiterum (the “app”) is a habit tracker developed by philschling (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses and how. In short: Habiterum is offline-first, has no user accounts, and we run no servers that store your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Your habits, completion history, reminders, and settings are stored only on your device using Android’s local storage. This data is not sent to us and we cannot see it. Any backup or export you create is started by you and saved to a location you choose.

2. Data processed by third parties

Advertising — Google AdMob. For users on the free version, the app shows a limited number of ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the Advertising ID), approximate (non-precise) location, and ad-interaction signals. This processing is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising terms. You can reset or limit the Advertising ID in your device settings. Buying Habiterum Pro removes all ads.

Purchases — Google Play Billing. Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; Google provides us only the purchase status needed to unlock Pro features on your device.

We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications — to show habit reminders.
  • Exact alarms — so reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup — to restore reminders after a reboot.
  • Ignore battery optimization (optional) — to keep reminders reliable.
  • Vibrate — reminder feedback.
  • Internet / network state — required for ads and purchases only.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or files beyond a backup file you explicitly choose.

4. Children

Habiterum is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

5. Selling or sharing of personal data

We do not sell your personal data. The only third-party processing is the advertising and payment services described above.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. There is no account to delete. For data processed by Google’s advertising or payment services, see Google’s privacy controls linked above. Questions? Contact us below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change accordingly.
